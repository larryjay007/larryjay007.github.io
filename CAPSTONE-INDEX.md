## The Thread

Everything above traces back to one instinct: **question a result before trusting it.**
That started with a construction-verification habit, showed up first in a "0 rows removed"
correlation check in Week 1, became the actual portfolio claim, then became the discipline
behind every leakage audit, every honest-split comparison, and the ML Study Coach's own
citation-fabrication catch. Same habit, applied at every scale, the whole way through.
